Terms

What you get

The Everything Pack is a one-time purchase that gives your household a licence to use every current Toot Toot Learning game and kit, plus every game we add to the pack in the future, on any devices in your home, for as long as you like. It is delivered as a download and a family code for the hosted family link.

What you can do

Play on every device in your home. Copy the files to a USB stick. Print the kits and worksheets as many times as you like for your own family. Share the storybook with your kids at bedtime.

What you can't do

Resell, rent or redistribute the files or your family code. Use one household purchase for a school or classroom (see the classroom licence). Remove our name from the games.

Classroom licence

One classroom licence covers one class of students under one teacher, on school devices, for the period sold. Students never need accounts. Larger school or district licences are available by email.

Refunds

If a game doesn't work on your device and we can't fix it within a week, we refund you. Email us with what device and browser you're using.

Free games

The free games are free for personal and classroom use. Please link to our site rather than re-hosting the files.
