Privacy

The short version: our games collect nothing. Our shop collects only what it needs to send you what you bought.

The games

The games have no accounts, no logins and no analytics. They do not connect to the internet while you play. Progress (which levels are unlocked, stars earned) is stored in your own browser on your own device, and you can clear it any time by clearing your browser data. If a child types their name into a personalized edition, that name stays inside the file on your device.

The shop

When you buy something, our payment provider (named on the checkout page) collects your email address and payment details to process the order. We receive your email address and what you bought so we can send your download and help you if something goes wrong. We do not sell or share this, and we do not add you to a mailing list unless you tick the box asking for one.

This website

This site uses no advertising or tracking cookies. If we use a simple visit counter, it will be one that stores no personal information.

Children

Our games are made for children, which is exactly why they collect nothing. Purchases are made by adults. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.
