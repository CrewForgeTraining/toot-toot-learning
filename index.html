Our promise to parents

Wholesome but silly. Never sticky.

Most kids’ games are designed to be hard to stop. Ours are designed to be easy to stop. Here is exactly what that means, in writing, for every game we make.

  • No ads, ever. Not even “kid-safe” ones.
  • No accounts, logins or passwords. Nothing leaves your device.
  • No streaks, timers, countdowns, daily rewards or notifications. Nothing ever says “come back”.
  • No in-game shop, coins, chests or random prizes. Stars come from getting it right, never from luck.
  • Levels never auto-advance. Every level ends with an “All done for today” button.
  • After three levels in a row, the game itself suggests a break.
  • Mistakes get a picture hint, then the answer. Nobody ever fails a level.
  • If your child goes quiet, the game repeats the instruction out loud and wiggles the thing to tap. No reading required.
  • The jokes are farts, bonks and a robot that goes boom. Never mean, never scary.
  • Works offline. Copy it to a USB stick if you like. Buy once and every future game is included.

Questions

Things parents ask

What do you collect about my child?

Nothing. The games have no accounts and send nothing anywhere. Progress is saved in your own browser. When you buy, we only get the email address you give the checkout so we can send your download.

Which devices does it work on?

Any browser from the last couple of years: Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox, on iPad, Android tablets, Chromebooks, Mac and Windows. On an iPad, open your family link in Safari and tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen" for an app icon.

Do I need internet?

Only to download. After that the games run offline. The USB gift box never needs internet at all.

Is it really one payment?

Yes. The Everything Pack is a one-time purchase and includes every game we release in the future. We may sell a separate second volume someday, but your pack stays complete.

Can I share it with my other kids?

Of course. One purchase covers every device in your home. Sharing with a whole school needs a classroom licence.

What ages is it for?

Designed for 5 to 8. Every game starts very easy and gets harder slowly, and a read-aloud button reads every instruction for kids who aren't reading yet.
